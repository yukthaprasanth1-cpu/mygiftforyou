A little letter for you ♡

I don't think I could ever fit everything I feel for you into one little birthday website, but I still wanted to try. When I think about us, I think about all those ordinary moments that somehow became some of my favourite memories. I loved spending time with your family and having you around my brothers too, watching movies together and staying up talking until two or three in the morning like sleep simply didn't exist.

I still think about the evenings when we'd sing together, and how I could hear you sing the same song a million times and somehow still want to hear it again. I loved cooking together too — although let's be honest, I mostly stood there watching you and taking videos because you barely let me do any actual work. 😭 And honestly, I didn't mind, because I loved having those little videos of you anyway.

You used to be there whenever I needed you, except for those moments when you were busy, and that meant more to me than I probably said out loud. And then there were the flowers. Whenever we went somewhere and you saw flowers, you'd pick one for me, and sometimes you'd collect six or seven of those tiny sunflower-looking flowers and turn them into the cutest little bouquet. Those tiny things made me ridiculously happy.

I still remember that first bouquet especially. The yellow flowers, the little purple flowers whose name I still don't know, the pink rose and the hibiscus. It wasn't about how big or expensive it was. It was special because you picked those flowers and gave them to me. That's the kind of thing I remember. The small things. The things that probably seemed completely ordinary to you, but somehow stayed with me.

And I hope you know that no matter who you become, no matter how much life changes you, I will always care about the person you are and the memories we made. You have been a very special part of my life, and I adore the way you sing, the way you make ordinary moments feel different, and all those little pieces of you that became familiar to me.

Also, before I finish this extremely emotional paragraph, I would like to officially remind you that I have been asking for cheesecake for FOUR MONTHS. Four. Whole. Months. And somehow I am still waiting. 😭🍰 But despite this completely unacceptable cheesecake situation, I still love and care about you very, very much.

So happy birthday. I hope this year is kind to you. I hope you get closer to the things you want, find reasons to laugh a little more, take care of yourself, and remember that you are deeply loved. Thank you for being part of so many of my favourite memories.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡
— me
