A Little Note for You ♡

Carino, I don't even know where to start because there are so many little memories of us that still make me smile. When I think about the time we've spent together, it's not only the big moments I remember. It's all the ordinary things that somehow became special because we were doing them together.

I remember spending time together with your family and my brothers, watching movies together and just being around each other. Those movie nights might sound simple, but they're the kind of memories that stay in my head for no reason at all — probably because they felt comfortable, happy and ours.

And then there were all those nights when we somehow kept talking and talking until 2 or 3 in the morning. We would start with one thing and suddenly hours had disappeared. I don't know how we managed to stay awake like that, but honestly, I loved those late-night conversations. 🌙

I also love remembering us cooking together. You never really let me do much work anyway. 😭 You would be busy cooking while I was mostly watching you and taking videos of you — because apparently one of my favourite hobbies is just recording you doing completely normal things. And I still love those videos because they hold tiny moments that I never want to forget.

And the evenings when we sang songs together… I adore those memories. Especially you singing for me. Even if I've heard the same song a million times, I would still want to hear you sing it again. There is just something about hearing a song from you that makes it feel different. 🎶

And I can never forget the flowers. Whenever we went somewhere and you saw flowers, you would pick one and give it to me. And when you found those tiny sunflower-like flowers, you would pick six or seven and make a little bouquet. It was such a small thing, but it made me ridiculously happy. 🌻

But the first bouquet you gave me will always have a special place in my heart. It was the first bouquet — the first flowers I received from you — and it was so beautiful. The yellow flowers, the purple flowers, the pink rose and the hibiscus… I remember all of it. 💐

You used to be there for me whenever I needed you, except for those times when you were busy, of course. And I want you to know that I remember that too. I remember the comfort of knowing that I could have you there, and I remember the little ways you showed that you cared.

There is also one extremely serious unfinished business between us: my cheesecake. 😭 I asked you for a cheesecake for four whole months and somehow I am STILL waiting. At this point, the cheesecake has become a historical event. But yes, despite this terrible betrayal, I still care about you and adore you. So congratulations, I guess. 😂🍰

No matter who you become, or how much you change, I think a part of me will always care about you and adore the person you are. I don't know what every future version of life will look like, but I know that the memories we've made have a special little place in my heart.

So on your birthday, I just want you to know that you are loved, you are cared for, and you matter to me more than a simple birthday message could ever explain. Thank you for all the little moments, the laughs, the late nights, the songs, the flowers, the movies, the cooking, and everything in between.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡ I hope this year gives you reasons to smile, reasons to feel proud of yourself, and a lot of beautiful moments to remember.

With love,
the person STILL waiting for her cheesecake. 😭🤍
