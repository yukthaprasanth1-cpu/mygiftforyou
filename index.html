Things I hope you always remember ♡

We've had so many little moments together that somehow became some of my favourite memories. I loved spending time with your family and having you around my brothers too, watching movies together, staying up talking until two or three in the morning, and just being together even when we weren't doing anything particularly special.

I still think about the evenings we spent singing together, and honestly, I could hear you sing the same song a million times and still love listening to you. I also love remembering the times we cooked together, even though I mostly ended up watching you and taking videos because you never really let me do much of the work. And yes, I absolutely loved taking those videos of you.

You used to be there whenever I needed you, except for the times you were busy, and that meant more to me than I probably ever said properly. And then there were the flowers. Whenever we went somewhere and you saw flowers, you'd pick one for me. Even those tiny little sunflower-looking flowers — you'd pick six or seven of them and somehow turn them into the cutest little bouquet.

I'll always remember the first bouquet especially. It had yellow flowers, purple flowers whose name I still don't know, a pink rose, and hibiscus. It was such a simple thing, but receiving flowers from you made it feel incredibly special. Those little gestures stayed with me.

And somehow, even after all this time, there is still one extremely important unfinished matter...

The cheesecake. 😭 Four months. I asked for cheesecake for FOUR MONTHS. And somehow I am still waiting. Honestly, the fact that I still care about you after that is proof that I really do love you. 😂🤍

But jokes aside, no matter who you become or where life takes you, I hope you always remember that you mattered to me. These memories are precious to me, and the time we shared will always have a special little place in my heart.

So on your birthday, I just hope you take care of yourself, be careful, enjoy your year, and make it a really good one. You deserve beautiful things, peaceful days, genuine happiness, and so many reasons to smile.

Happy birthday. ♡

And I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🍰

Happy Birthday, Carina

You are loved more than you imagine.
