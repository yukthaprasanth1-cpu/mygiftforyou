A letter for you ♡

I don't even know where to begin because somehow there are so many little things I remember about us. Not just the big moments, but the completely ordinary ones that somehow became my favourite memories. Spending time with your family and with my brothers, watching movies together, staying awake talking until two or three in the morning, cooking together, and just being around each other are all things I keep close to my heart.

I especially remember our evenings when we'd sing together. I could hear you sing the same song a million times and I would still want to hear you sing it again. I genuinely adore your singing that much. And whenever we cooked together, I mostly ended up watching you and taking videos because you barely let me do any work anyway. 😭 Honestly, I didn't mind. I loved watching you do your thing and keeping those tiny moments on my phone.

You also used to be there whenever I needed you, except for those times when you were busy, of course. And one of the sweetest things I remember is how you used to pick flowers for me whenever we went somewhere. If you saw flowers, you'd pick one and give it to me. Sometimes you'd find those tiny sunflower-looking flowers and pick six or seven of them just to make me a tiny little bouquet.

And I'll never forget that first bouquet. It had yellow flowers, purple flowers that I still don't know the name of, a pink rose, and hibiscus. It might have looked like a simple little collection of flowers, but to me it was something incredibly special because it was the first bouquet I received from you.

There are so many things I could keep talking about, because every memory somehow has its own little feeling attached to it. Even the silly things make me smile. Like the fact that I asked you for cheesecake for four whole months and somehow I am STILL waiting. Four months, Carino. 😭🍰 And somehow, despite that very serious crime, I still care about you and love you anyway.

I don't know exactly where life will take either of us or who we'll become as time goes on. But I do know that the memories we made mattered to me. You mattered to me. And no matter who you become, I hope you always remember that there was someone who genuinely cared about you, adored the little things about you, loved hearing you sing, loved your flowers, loved our late-night conversations, and loved simply being around you.

So on your birthday, I just want you to have a beautiful year. Take care of yourself. Be careful. Eat properly, rest when you need to, keep doing the things that make you happy, and don't forget how loved you are.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡ I hope this year gives you so many reasons to smile. And yes... I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🤍
