A little letter 🤍

I don't think I could ever put all of our memories into one little letter, but I still wanted to try. When I think about the time we've spent together, it's always the little things that come back to me first.

I loved spending time with your family and having you around my brothers too. I loved our movie nights, the random conversations, and all those times we somehow ended up staying awake talking until two or three in the morning. Those late-night talks are memories I know I'll always keep close.

And then there was cooking together. Well... technically, you cooking and me mostly watching you and taking videos. 😭 You never even let me do much work anyway. But honestly, I loved standing there watching you, recording those tiny moments, and just being there with you.

I also loved our evenings when we'd sing together. I genuinely adore hearing you sing. You could sing the same song a million times, and somehow I'd still want to hear you sing it one more time. There is just something about your voice that I could never get tired of.

You were also there for me whenever I needed you, except for those moments when you were busy, obviously. And I loved how you'd bring me flowers whenever you went out. Even if you saw a tiny flower somewhere, you'd pick it and give it to me. Sometimes you'd pick six or seven of those tiny sunflower-like flowers and make a little bouquet. Those little bouquets meant so much to me.

I'll always remember the first bouquet especially. The yellow flowers, the little purple flowers whose name I still don't know, the pink rose, and the hibiscus. It was such a simple thing, but it became one of those memories that somehow stayed with me.

And of course, I cannot write this letter without mentioning the greatest tragedy of all: the cheesecake. 😭 I have been asking for cheesecake for FOUR MONTHS. FOUR. MONTHS. And yet somehow I am still here, still waiting, still loving you despite this betrayal.

Jokes aside, no matter who you become, where life takes you, or how much things change, I hope you always know that you mattered to me deeply. I adore the memories we've made, the silly little moments, the quiet ones, the laughter, the songs, the flowers, the movies, the late nights... all of it.

I hope this year is kind to you. I hope you take care of yourself, stay safe, laugh a lot, find things that make you happy, and become everything you want to become. And I hope somewhere in all of those future memories, you remember that there was someone who cared about you so, so much.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡
And yes... I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🍰
