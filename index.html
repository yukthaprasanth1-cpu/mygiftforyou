A Little Note for You ♡

Carino, I don't even know where to start because there are so many little memories of us that still make me smile. When I think about the time we've spent together, it's not only the big moments that I remember. It's all the ordinary little things that somehow became special just because we were doing them together.

I remember spending time together with your family and my brothers, watching movies together and just being around each other. Those moments might sound simple, but they're some of the memories that stayed with me the most. There was something so comfortable about just being together and doing nothing particularly special.

And then there were all those nights when we somehow kept talking and talking until 2 or 3 in the morning. We would start talking about one thing and suddenly hours had disappeared. I honestly don't know how we managed to stay awake like that, but I loved those late-night conversations. Those quiet hours became some of my favourite memories.

I also love remembering us cooking together. Well… technically, you were doing most of the cooking. 😭 You never really let me do much work anyway. I would mostly stand there watching you and taking videos of you, because apparently one of my favourite hobbies is recording you doing completely normal things. And honestly, I still love those videos because they're little pieces of moments I never want to forget.

And the evenings when we sang songs together… I adore those memories. Especially you singing. Even if I've heard the same song a million times, I could still listen to you sing it again. There is something about hearing you sing that makes the song feel different, and I don't think I'll ever get tired of it. 🎶

I can never forget the flowers either. Whenever we went somewhere and you saw flowers, you would pick one and give it to me. And those tiny sunflower-like flowers… you would pick six or seven of them and make a tiny little bouquet. It was such a small thing, but it made me ridiculously happy. 🌻

And that first bouquet will always be especially special to me. It was the first bouquet, the first flowers I received from you. There were yellow flowers, purple flowers whose name I still don't know, a pink rose and hibiscus. I still remember it because it wasn't really about how big or expensive the bouquet was. It was the fact that you gave it to me.

You used to be there for me whenever I needed you, except for those times when you were busy, of course. And I want you to know that I remember those little things too. I remember the comfort of knowing that I could have you there, and I remember all the little ways you showed that you cared.

There is also one extremely serious unfinished business between us: my cheesecake. 😭 I asked you for a cheesecake for four whole months and somehow I am STILL waiting. Four months. At this point, that cheesecake is basically part of our history. But somehow, despite this terrible betrayal, I still care about you and adore you. So I guess I'll survive. 😂🍰

No matter who you become, or how much life changes you, I hope you always know that you mattered to me deeply. I don't know what every future version of life will look like, but I know that the memories we've made have a special little place in my heart.

So on your birthday, I just want you to know that you are loved, you are cared for, and you matter to me more than a simple birthday message could ever explain. Thank you for all the little moments — the laughs, the late nights, the movies, the cooking, the singing, the flowers, the random conversations and everything in between.

I hope this year gives you reasons to smile, reasons to be proud of yourself, beautiful memories, peaceful days and everything good that you deserve.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡
I hope you always know how loved you are.

With love,
the person STILL waiting for her cheesecake. 😭🤍
