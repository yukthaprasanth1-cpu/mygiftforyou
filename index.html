For you, Carino ♡

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡ When I think about us, I think about all those little moments that somehow became some of my favourite memories. Spending time with your family and my brothers, watching movies together, staying up talking until two or three in the morning, and just being around each other made ordinary days feel really special. I loved our little evenings of singing too, especially because I could listen to you sing the same song a million times and still never get tired of it. I genuinely adore hearing you sing, even when you've already sung the exact same thing so many times.

I also love remembering the times we cooked together. Well... when I say "cooked together," I mostly mean I watched you, took videos of you, and tried to help while you somehow never let me do much work. 😭♡ You were there for me whenever I needed you, except for those moments when you were busy, and that meant more to me than I probably said. And then there were the flowers. Whenever we went somewhere and you saw flowers, you would pick one for me, and even the tiny little sunflower-like flowers became a cute little bouquet when you picked six or seven of them together. I still remember that first bouquet so clearly: the yellow flowers, the purple ones whose name I still don't know, the pink rose, and the hibiscus. It was such a simple thing, but it became one of those memories I don't want to lose.

No matter who you become, where life takes you, or how much things change, I hope you always remember that you mattered deeply to me and that I will always care about the person you are. I hope this year gives you beautiful moments, peaceful days, reasons to laugh, and everything you genuinely deserve. I hope you take care of yourself, stay careful, and have the kind of year that makes you look back and feel proud and happy. And yes, after all this emotional stuff, I have one extremely serious issue to mention...

I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🍰 Four months. FOUR. MONTHS. Anyway, happy birthday, Carino. I hope you know how loved you are, how much your presence has meant to me, and how many little pieces of happiness you've left behind in my memories. ♡
