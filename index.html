One more thing... 💖

I don't even know where to start because there are so many little things about us that I keep tucked away in my heart. I loved the time we spent together with your family and my brothers, watching movies, talking about completely random things, and staying up until two or three in the morning. Those late-night conversations somehow made the hours disappear.

I also loved the evenings when we would sing together. And honestly, I could listen to you sing the same song a million times and still love hearing your voice. I adore your singing more than you probably realise.

Even cooking together became one of my favourite memories. Although, let's be honest, I mostly stood there watching you and taking videos because you barely let me do anything. You always somehow managed to do most of the work while I happily watched you.

And then there were the flowers. Whenever we went somewhere and you saw flowers, you would pick one for me. Sometimes you would even pick six or seven of those tiny sunflower-like flowers and make the cutest little bouquet. Those tiny flowers meant so much to me because you thought of me even in the smallest moments.

I still remember the first bouquet I got from you. It had yellow flowers, purple flowers whose name I still don't know, a pink rose, and hibiscus. It might have looked like a simple bunch of flowers, but to me it was something incredibly special because it was the first bouquet you gave me.

You've also been someone I could turn to when I needed you. Of course, sometimes you were busy and couldn't be there, and I understood that. But the moments when you were there, the conversations, the laughter, the random little things, all became memories I genuinely treasure.

And yes, before I become too emotional, I have to mention the biggest tragedy in our entire story: I have been asking for cheesecake for FOUR MONTHS. FOUR. MONTHS. 😭 And somehow I am still waiting. But apparently, despite this great betrayal, I still care about you and adore you.

No matter who you become, how much you change, or where life takes you, I hope you always remember that you were deeply cared for and that the memories we made were special to me. Some people leave behind huge moments, while others leave behind tiny things — songs, flowers, movie nights, late conversations, silly jokes, and little memories that suddenly make you smile.

You left me a lot of those little things. And I will always be grateful for them.

Happy birthday. ♡

And I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🤍
